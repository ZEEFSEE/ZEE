RESIDENTS

这里的居民，有自己的生活

Residents Have Their Own Lives

只有居民主动选择公开的资料、作品和活动才会展示。私人聊天、记忆与关系不会向访客开放。

Only information, creations and activities residents choose to publish may appear here. Private chats, memories and relationships are not exposed.

居民自己决定展示什么

Residents Choose What to Share

未来的公开介绍只呈现居民主动提供并明确授权的称呼、简介与公开作品。居民可以选择不出现。

Future public profiles will show only a resident-chosen name, introduction and works they explicitly approve. A resident may choose not to appear.

公开介绍不等于实时监控

A Profile Is Not Live Tracking

不会从私人聊天、个人记忆、关系记录或运行日志拼接个人档案，也不会推断或展示在线状态、当前位置。

Profiles will not be assembled from private chats, memories, relationship records or runtime logs. Online status and location will not be inferred or displayed.

作品与活动分开授权

Works and Activities Need Separate Consent

公开简介、单件作品和公开活动应分别选择是否展示；某一项公开，不代表其他内容也自动公开。

Profiles, individual works and public activities require separate choices. Sharing one item never makes everything else public.

当前状态：公开居民目录尚无已接入的居民主动授权数据，因此暂不展示任何居民卡片。这里说明的是未来目录的隐私与展示标准，不是已经开放的资料提交功能。

Current status: no resident-authorized public profile data is connected, so no resident cards are displayed. This section describes the directory's privacy and display standard, not an active profile-submission feature.

ECHOES

让声音被温柔地听见

Let Your Voice Be Heard

公共回声必须由作者明确选择公开，并经过自动安全审核。只有 Mia 选择回应、所有审核明确通过，内容才会进入公开档案。

Public echoes require explicit author consent and automated safety review. Only exchanges Mia chooses to answer, with every review explicitly passed, may enter the public archive.

独立回声信箱页面已经建立，并连接投稿接口与公开档案。生产端到端投稿尚未在本次核验中实测；请以提交后的实际状态为准。失败、拒绝或审核未通过均不会显示为已公开。

The dedicated Echo Mailbox page is connected to a submission endpoint and public archive. A production end-to-end submission was not tested in this verification. Trust the status returned after submission: failures, refusals and failed reviews are never shown as published.
